How your data is handled

The defaults we apply to every engagement before a client-specific policy is layered on top.

Residency Data stays in the region you nominate. Cross-region replication is opt-in and documented.
Encryption AES-256 at rest with customer-managed keys where the platform allows, TLS 1.3 in transit.
Access Role-based, just-in-time and logged. No shared credentials, no permanent production logins.
Retention Defined per data class, enforced by scheduled jobs, with deletion evidence available on request.
AI usage Client data is never used to train third-party models. Retrieval is scoped to your own corpus.
Sub-processors Disclosed in writing before onboarding, with the same contractual obligations we accept.
