How an engagement runs

A fixed sequence, so scope and ownership never drift once delivery starts.

Discovery & architecture review

A 30-minute call with a principal engineer, followed by a written review of your current stack, constraints and the risks that block the next release.

Technical delivery plan

Scope broken into two-week slices with owners, dependencies and acceptance criteria. You approve the plan before anyone writes code.

Squad build

Your squad ships into your repositories and pipelines. Weekly demos, continuous review and observability from the first release onward.

Handover & hardening

Documentation, runbooks and load testing, then a staged handover to your team — or a retainer if you want the squad to keep owning uptime.
