Case study — fintech reconciliation

The engagement that best shows the four tracks combining in one squad: AI, cloud, full-stack and modernization.

Client profile A multi-market payments provider reconciling transactions across three acquirer feeds. Named reference available under NDA.
Constraint Settlement review ran overnight in batch. A single unmatched line could hold up the whole close, and nobody could explain a match without reading raw logs.
What we built An event-driven ingestion path, matching agents that score candidate pairs, an explainable audit trail per decision, and a review queue for the exceptions the agents would not confirm.
Outcome Settlement review dropped from hours to minutes. Every match is now explainable without a log dive.
Stack Go services, Kafka, PostgreSQL with pgvector, Kubernetes on AWS, Terraform, OpenTelemetry.
